## Why

Madeireira Cambirela scores 52.4/100 in the benchmark — classified as "Fraco" despite having the highest recommendation score (8.0/10) among low-scoring competitors. The business is well-regarded (20+ years, active social media, customer testimonials, broad product catalog) but the website fails to convert that reputation into leads. Critical problems:

- **ZERO forms** — no contact form, no quote form, no lead capture mechanism whatsoever
- **3 different WhatsApp numbers** scattered across the site — confusing for visitors
- **Meta description ABSENT** on all pages — invisible to search snippet previews
- **Title tag generic** ("MADEIREIRA CAMBIRELA") — identical on all URLs, no keywords or location
- **Zero schema.org structured data** despite running WordPress with Elementor
- **Majority of 30+ images have empty alt text** — total loss of image SEO
- **Instagram link malformed** (`http://@madeireiracambirela`) — broken URL in footer
- **Only 8 product images for 25+ products** — no individual product pages or photos

The paradox: customers love the business (8.0/10 rec), but the site cannot capture new leads (10.7/25 conv). The rebuild closes this gap.

## What Changes

- Add a quote request form with product selection, name, phone/WhatsApp, and message fields.
- Unify WhatsApp to 1 primary number with internal routing, replacing the 3 scattered numbers.
- Create individual product cards with photos, descriptions, and per-product quote CTAs.
- Add meta descriptions, unique title tags per section, and JSON-LD LocalBusiness structured data.
- Fix all image alt text with descriptive, keyword-rich content.
- Fix the broken Instagram link to the correct URL (`https://instagram.com/madeireiracambirela`).
- Preserve existing strengths: testimonials section, gallery, company history, warm visual identity.
- Keep the existing responsive layout foundation (Elementor already provides media queries).
- Keep e-commerce checkout, online payment, and customer accounts out of scope.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `modern-responsive-site`: Defines the improved responsive layout, product showcase with individual cards, navigation with quote CTA, and overall site structure replacing the current flat product list.
- `seo-and-discoverability`: Defines metadata, structured data, heading hierarchy fixes, image alt text, and title tag improvements to fix the zero-metadata baseline.
- `conversion-and-lead-capture`: Defines quote forms, unified WhatsApp strategy, CTAs, and lead capture flows to close the conversion gap (10.7 -> 18+/25).

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Preserves existing content and visual identity — this is an enhancement, not a full ground-up rebuild.
- Requires product photos for the 17 core products (currently only ~8 images for 25+ items).
- Requires business decision on which WhatsApp number to use as primary.
- Does not require checkout, payment processing, inventory systems, or customer login.
- Expected score improvement: 52.4 -> 70+/100, with largest gains in Conversion (+7) and SEO (+6).
