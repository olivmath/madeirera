## Why

Palhoca Casas de Madeira scores 40.5/100 in the benchmark — ranked "Fraco" among 20+ lumber yards in Grande Florianopolis. The site is a single-page WordPress/Elementor build with minimal content. Critical problems:

- **Duplicate H1 tags** — "CASAS DE MADEIRA NA PALHOCA" and "Vamos comecar?" both as H1, breaking heading hierarchy
- **Zero meta description, zero Open Graph, zero schema.org** — SEO score is 2.0/19.5
- **Zero forms** — entire lead capture depends exclusively on WhatsApp links
- **Only 5 images total, all with empty alt text** — no product catalog, no gallery, no house model showcase
- **Single page with minimal content** — no dedicated pages for house models, no blog, no social media links
- **No house model catalog** — despite being a pre-fabricated house specialist, zero models are displayed with specs or pricing

The business has strong differentiators worth surfacing: 20 years of experience, Caixa Economica Federal financing acceptance, extended hours (08:00-22:00 including weekends), autoclave-treated pinus construction, and architect-designed custom projects.

## What Changes

- Replace the single-page Elementor site with a structured, multi-section responsive site.
- Add a hero section emphasizing the pre-fabricated wooden house niche, Caixa financing badge, and primary CTA in the first viewport.
- Add a house model showcase with at least 4-6 pre-fab house types, floor plans, specs, and pricing ranges.
- Add a product catalog for lumber types (pinus, eucalipto, angelim, madeiras tratadas).
- Add a quote request form and a financing inquiry form — currently zero forms exist.
- Fix heading hierarchy: exactly one H1 per page, structured H2/H3 sections.
- Add meta description, Open Graph metadata, and JSON-LD structured data (LocalBusiness, Product).
- Add descriptive alt text to all images — currently all 5 images have empty alt.
- Add a photo gallery of completed projects (the "Fotos" section exists but has only 1 project image).
- Keep the existing WhatsApp integration (already well-placed in header, hero, footer) and enhance with contextual messages per house model.
- Keep e-commerce checkout, online payment, and customer accounts out of scope.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `modern-responsive-site`: Defines the responsive layout, mobile-first design, house model showcase, gallery, navigation, hero section, footer, and overall site structure enhancing the current minimal single-page layout.
- `seo-and-discoverability`: Defines semantic HTML, metadata, structured data, heading hierarchy fix (duplicate H1), image alt text, and crawlability requirements.
- `conversion-and-lead-capture`: Defines quote forms, financing inquiry form, CTAs, WhatsApp enhancement with model context, and lead capture flows to eliminate the current zero-form dependency on WhatsApp alone.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Restructures the entire site content from a single minimal page into a proper multi-section site with house model catalog.
- Requires house model photos, floor plans, and pricing ranges (or "a partir de" ranges) for the pre-fab house catalog.
- Requires confirmation of current house models offered, pricing, and any updated contact details.
- Does not require checkout, payment processing, inventory systems, or customer login.
- Expected score improvement: 40.5 -> 70+/100, with major gains in SEO (2.0 -> 14+), Conversao (13.8 -> 20+), and Recomendacao (2.0 -> 7+).
