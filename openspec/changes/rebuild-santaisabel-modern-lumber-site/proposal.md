## Why

Madeireira Santa Isabel scores 23.1/100 in the benchmark — rated "Critico" among 20+ lumber yards in Grande Florianopolis. The site was built with Adobe Muse, which Adobe discontinued in 2018. The CMS is dead — no updates, no security patches, no way to maintain it. Critical problems:

- **Adobe Muse discontinued in 2018** — CMS is dead, site is frozen in time, impossible to maintain or update
- **Layout uses absolute positioning** — zero responsive behavior, no viewport meta tag, completely unusable on mobile
- **Zero H1 or H2 headings** — semantic hierarchy is nonexistent, devastating for SEO
- **57 images with zero alt text** (100% of all images) — accessibility and SEO failure
- **Zero forms, broken WhatsApp widget** — no way to request a quote or contact the business digitally
- **Zero schema.org markup** — invisible to structured search
- **Design circa 2010** — severely dated visual identity that undermines the company's 80-year heritage

Despite these problems, the business has exceptional content worth preserving: heritage since 1945 (80+ years), family succession story, 1,200m2 facility with 525m2 built area, pre-fabricated houses in wood and masonry, reforestation/sustainability commitment, active social media (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram), show room with completed projects, and accepts multiple financing options (Construcard, Construshop, credit cards).

## What Changes

- Replace the entire Adobe Muse site with a modern, mobile-first responsive site.
- Add a hero section showcasing the 1945 heritage, pre-fabricated house specialization, and primary CTAs in the first viewport.
- Add a project/product showcase with house categories (wood, masonry/concrete) and photo galleries from the show room.
- Add a quote request form with project type context, name, phone, city, and message fields.
- Add semantic HTML structure: H1 per page, H2 sections, alt text for all images (fixing the 57-image deficit).
- Add meta descriptions, Open Graph metadata, canonical URLs, and JSON-LD structured data (LocalBusiness).
- Add a working WhatsApp button with contextual prefilled messages per page/project type.
- Integrate the sustainability/reforestation content as a trust signal and differentiator.
- Keep the existing contact info (phone, email, address, business hours) visible in header/footer.
- Keep e-commerce checkout, online payment, and customer accounts out of scope.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `modern-responsive-site`: Defines the responsive layout, mobile-first design, navigation, heritage-focused hero section, project showcase, footer, and overall site structure replacing the 2010 Adobe Muse fixed-width layout.
- `seo-and-discoverability`: Defines semantic HTML, metadata, structured data, headings hierarchy, image alt text (fixing 57 images), and crawlability requirements to fix the zero-SEO baseline.
- `conversion-and-lead-capture`: Defines quote forms, CTAs, working WhatsApp integration, phone click-to-call, and lead capture flows to replace the current zero-conversion state.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.
