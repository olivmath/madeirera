## Why

Madecor scores 41.2/100 in the benchmark, with the largest gaps in product depth, SEO, trust signals, technical quality, and digital maturity. The site should become a premium commercial channel for a local madeireira: easy to trust, easy to understand, easy to quote, and easy for search engines and AI assistants to parse.

## What Changes

- Reposition the site as a premium institutional and commercial experience for Madecor in Palhoca and Grande Florianopolis.
- Add a structured product catalog with categories, filters, product detail pages, technical specifications, usage guidance, and quote CTAs.
- Add a quote flow that supports both contextual WhatsApp and a structured form per product/category.
- Add trust-building content: real projects, testimonials/reviews, company credentials, business identity, location, hours, service region, and professional contact identity.
- Add search and AI discoverability foundations: semantic HTML, canonical HTTPS URLs, metadata, sitemap, `llms.txt`, structured data, FAQ content, and crawlable pages by search intent.
- Improve mobile-first UX, accessibility, image handling, and Core Web Vitals so the site can compete with the benchmark leaders.
- Keep e-commerce checkout, online payment, real-time stock, and customer accounts out of scope for this version.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `premium-institutional-experience`: Defines the premium public site experience, positioning, trust signals, navigation, mobile UX, and conversion surfaces for Madecor.
- `advanced-commercial-catalog`: Defines catalog, category, product detail, filtering, product education, and quote intent behavior.
- `search-ai-discoverability`: Defines SEO, local discovery, structured data, AI-readable resources, FAQ, sitemap, and crawlability requirements.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Affects the future website information architecture, page templates, navigation, content model, SEO metadata, structured data, analytics events, and lead capture flows.
- Requires product/category content, product images, company trust assets, local business data, testimonials or review excerpts, service-area details, and contact/channel configuration.
- Does not require checkout, online payment, inventory synchronization, customer login, or ERP integration in this version.
